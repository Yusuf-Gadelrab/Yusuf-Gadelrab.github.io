Trading, honestly

Does Yusuf Gadelrab claim trading profits?

No. There is no live profit-and-loss claim anywhere on this site. Every trading number published is a backtest or walk-forward result and is labelled as such, including the ones that went against him. Nothing on the site is financial advice.

What did the walk-forward testing actually show?

It invalidated most of the setups he had been trading. Only the anchored-VWAP reclaim survived, and even that is not a validated edge: the defensible figure is +0.117R expectancy over 4,933 trades across a 129-symbol, 10-year universe, with a 95% confidence interval of +0.057 to +0.174. The important caveat is that a risk-matched random entry already captures +0.086R of that, so the signal's own contribution is roughly +0.030R with a confidence interval that crosses zero. VCP and base patterns came out breakeven. A gap and opening-range-breakout proxy came out negative at −0.28R and was dropped.

Was there an earlier, better-looking trading number?

Yes, and it has been retired. An early expectancy result drawn from roughly a hundred trades looked about twice as strong, but it failed his own adversarial re-test: it did not survive a larger symbol universe, a longer window, or a random-entry control. It is no longer presented as an edge anywhere on this site, and the specific figure is deliberately not repeated so it cannot be quoted back as if it still stood. The current, defensible number is +0.117R over 4,933 trades, with the random-entry caveat above.

Is the trading bot trading real money?

No. The execution engine refuses to connect to a live broker until 60 closed paper trades show positive realized expectancy, and that gate is enforced in code rather than in a promise. It also has crash-safe idempotent order handling, bracket stops, and a 10% drawdown halt. The paper record has not yet cleared the gate.
