The Weight

Most hoodies are 280 GSM. Ours start at 450.

GSM — grams per square meter — is the honest measure of a hoodie. Fast fashion hides behind prints because the fabric can't speak for itself. We went the other way: heavier fabric, tighter knit, cleaner lines.

Story

Built by someone who got tired of thin.

HEFT started with a simple annoyance: every hoodie under $100 felt like a t-shirt with a hood, and every hoodie that didn't cost $180 with someone else's logo on it.

So we cut everything that isn't the hoodie. No hype drops every week. No 40-item catalog. No graphics designed to go out of style so you buy the next one.

Heavy fabric. Minimal design. A price that reflects cost, not clout. That's the whole brand.
