Apply OS

An application tracker with a deadline radar — for internships, scholarships, grants and fellowships. The spreadsheet you keep meaning to update, except it tells you what is due next and never loses a recommender.

Import / export

Your data stays on this device. Everything is saved in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and there is no server. Clearing your browser data — or opening the page in a different browser or in private mode — means starting empty, so export a JSON backup if the list matters to you.

The headline feature

Deadline radar

What is actually due, grouped by how much time is left. Dates are compared calendar-day to calendar-day, so nothing shifts because of a timezone.
