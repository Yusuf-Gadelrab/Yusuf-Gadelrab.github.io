02 · The seven

What this place is, stated plainly

  1. Receipts over opinionsIn the proof channels, show the work. Takes without receipts go in the discussion channels. Small receipts count — "I wrote twenty lines and they work" counts.
  2. Nobody sells signals hereNo paid picks, no "DM me for entries", no copy-trading, no affiliate dumps. First offence is a ban, not a warning.
  3. None of it is adviceNothing posted is financial, legal, immigration, or medical advice. Traders share journals. Students share notes. You own your decisions.
  4. Give two, take oneWant a resume review, a code review, or trade feedback? Give two others the same first. That ratio is the entire economy of the room.
  5. No private pitchingDon't cold-DM members to sell, recruit, or promote. Post it in the open or don't post it.
  6. Failure ranks equal to winsRejections, blown stops, dead projects — post them. A room where only wins are visible teaches nothing and makes people lie.
  7. Be someone people want in the roomNo slurs, no harassment, no doxxing, no NSFW. One strike.

03 · The rituals

The reason it stays alive

Communities don't die from lack of members. They die from having nothing that happens on a schedule.

DAILY · 08:00 PT
Daily Fire

One line: what are you shipping today? Next morning, you report whether you did it. No goals, no plans — one shippable thing.

FRIDAY · 16:00 PT
The Floor

Open voice call, no agenda. Screenshare something broken, ask about a trade, or just work quietly with the call on. Ten minutes counts.

SUNDAY · 10:00 PT
Weekly Wins

One win, one lesson, one ask. The asks get answered — that's the actual point of the thread.

MONTHLY
Build Sprint

48 hours, any lane, ship something that works end to end. Demo call on Sunday, five minutes each, screenshare, no slides.

FAQ

Common questions

What is DHAHAB Circle?

A free private community for people working across four lanes at once: building software and AI, trading markets, pursuing internships, and running a creative brand. The defining rule is that members post proof of work rather than opinions.

How much does it cost to join?

Nothing. There is no paid tier, no course at the end, and no upsell. The first 50 members keep a permanent founding role.

Does the community sell trading signals?

No, and it never will. Selling signals, paid picks, or copy-trading is grounds for immediate removal. Members share journals and closed-trade reviews as education. Nothing shared is financial advice.

Who is it for?

Students and early-career builders who ship things: developers learning AI, traders who journal their results, international students navigating internships and visas, and creators building a brand. It suits people doing several of those at once.

What do I have to do after joining?

Two things: post an introduction and pick your lanes. That unlocks the rest of the server. After that, the only expectation is that you post receipts of real work rather than opinions.
