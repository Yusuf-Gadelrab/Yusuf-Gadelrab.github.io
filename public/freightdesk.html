Questions Brokers Ask

Before you book a pilot.

What does FreightDesk AI automate?

FreightDesk covers the full 18-point workload of a small-freight financial controller: POD requests and verification, invoice disputes, rate reconciliation, advance reconciliation, W9 tracking, 30-day past-due follow-up, release-list generation, AR aging, and reply drafting for every incoming billing/AR email.

Will FreightDesk AI send emails without my approval?

Not unless you enable it. Every draft starts in one-click-approval mode — you read it, you send it. Once you trust a category, such as routine POD replies, you can flip that category to auto-send. You decide per workflow, never all-or-nothing.

Does my data go to the cloud?

No. FreightDesk runs on a local AI model on your own machine. Rates, margins, customer lists, and disputes never leave your building, and there are no per-message API fees.
