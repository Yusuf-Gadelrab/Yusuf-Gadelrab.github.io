# Freelance Business OS — Preview

- **A real app, not a Notion clone-and-hope-it-works template.** One HTML file you open by double-clicking — no Notion account, no subscription, no vendor who can raise prices or shut down. Clients, pipeline, projects, invoices, time tracking, and goals in one dashboard that actually persists your data.
- **Built for how freelancers actually get paid.** A weighted pipeline that shows real expected revenue (not just a wishlist), invoices that auto-flag overdue by date instead of you tracking it manually, and a time log that tells you your unbilled value at a glance.
- **You own the file, and you own the data.** Everything saves to your browser, exports to JSON (full backup/transfer) or CSV (invoices, for your accountant) with one click — nothing lives on anyone's server but yours.

**Suggested price: $59**
