Executive Summary

A 90-day path to a self-running back office

Cascade Freight Brokers moves 340+ loads a month with a three-person back office still keying rate confirmations and check calls by hand. This proposal outlines a focused pilot to remove that manual layer without disrupting live freight.

FreightDesk AI reads inbound rate confirmations, carrier check-in emails, and factoring paperwork, then writes clean, structured data directly into Cascade's existing TMS and accounting stack — no rip-and-replace, no new software for dispatchers to learn.

The engagement is structured as a phased pilot: a two-week Discovery to map your exact document formats and exception cases, a three-week Build against a live sandbox, and a guided Launch with your team watching every automated decision before it goes hands-off.

Every hour your team spends re-keying a rate confirmation is an hour not spent booking the next load. FreightDesk AI removes that hour — permanently.

Understanding Your Operation

Three bottlenecks costing Cascade time and margin

Findings below are drawn from the July 22 operations walkthrough with Marcus Reyes and two dispatchers.

1

Rate confirmations are re-typed by hand into the TMS

Each of the ~85 weekly rate-cons arrives as a PDF or forwarded email and is manually keyed into Cascade's TMS by a dispatcher, introducing transposition errors on rate, accessorials, and reference numbers.

~7 hrs/week
2

Carrier check-calls are logged inconsistently

Check-in emails and texts land across three inboxes and one shared phone line, with no single source of truth — customers occasionally get conflicting status updates on the same load.

~3 hrs/week
3

Invoice reconciliation lags by 4–6 business days

Matching signed BOLs and carrier invoices to the original rate confirmation is done at week's end in a batch, delaying factoring submissions and tying up cash Cascade could otherwise turn faster.

4–6 day delay

Proposed Solution

A phased rollout, built to de-risk change

No dispatcher workflow changes until your team has approved the automation's output in a live sandbox. Each phase ends with a go/no-go checkpoint.

Discovery

Aug 4 – Aug 15, 2026

Document-format mapping, TMS & accounting API access, exception-case catalog, success-metric sign-off.

Build

Aug 18 – Sep 5, 2026

Rate-con parser, check-call unifier, and invoice-matching pipeline built against a sandboxed copy of your TMS.

Launch

Sep 8 – Sep 19, 2026

Parallel-run against live loads, dispatcher walkthrough, staged cutover, and a 30-day tuning window.

Why phased: freight brokerages run on trust between dispatch and carriers — a mis-keyed rate or a dropped check-call has real cost. Running the pilot in parallel with your existing process for the first two weeks of Launch means nothing goes live until your own team has verified it.

Scope of Work

What's included — and what isn't

We'd rather over-communicate boundaries now than surprise you at invoice time.

In Scope

WorkstreamDescription
Rate-con parsingAutomated extraction of rate, lane, reference #, and accessorials from PDF/email rate confirmations into the TMS.
Check-call unificationSingle status feed combining carrier email, SMS, and phone-logged check-ins into one load-status record.
Invoice matchingAutomated 3-way match of rate confirmation, signed BOL, and carrier invoice, flagging discrepancies for review.
TMS & accounting integrationRead/write integration with Cascade's existing TMS and accounting platform via named APIs identified in Discovery.
Dispatcher trainingOne live walkthrough session and a written quick-reference guide for the dispatch team.

Out of Scope

ItemWhy it's excluded
Custom TMS developmentThis pilot integrates with your existing TMS — it does not modify or extend the TMS platform itself.
Carrier-side integrations beyond the 3 named APIsAdditional carrier or broker-network integrations can be scoped as a change order once Discovery confirms demand.
After-hours live supportPilot support is business-hours (8am–6pm PT). 24/7 monitoring is available as a post-pilot add-on.
Historical data backfill beyond 90 daysKeeps Discovery scoped to a two-week window; older records can be migrated separately if needed.
