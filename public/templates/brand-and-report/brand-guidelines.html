Halloway Studio was founded on a simple premise: freight brokers and logistics operators deserve marketing that moves as fast and as precisely as their trucks do. Halloway Studio · CONFIDENTIAL05
Verbal Identity

Voice & Tone

Confident and specific, never hypey. We write like an operator who has been in the room, not a marketer performing for one.

SituationDoDon't
Describing results"Cut quote turnaround from 4 hours to 22 minutes.""Revolutionized their entire workflow overnight."
Talking about ourselves"We build the systems; you keep the relationships.""We're a passionate team of storytellers and disruptors."
Setting expectations"Expect the first draft in 5 business days.""We'll get back to you ASAP!!"
Handling a miss"That slipped. Here's the fix and the new date.""Due to unforeseen circumstances beyond our control..."
Selling"Founding-client pricing closes Aug 15.""LIMITED TIME OFFER — ACT NOW!"
Sign-offs"Questions before we start? Reply here.""Please don't hesitate to reach out! :)"
Halloway Studio · CONFIDENTIAL06
Visual Direction

Photography

Photography reinforces the same restraint as the rest of the system — real operations, low saturation, high trust.

Yard & dock, golden hour
Operators at work, candid
Detail shots, texture & grit
  • Desaturate 15–20% before grading a warm gold tone into the highlights.
  • Shoot real operations — real drivers, real docks. No stock-photo handshakes.
  • Low, directional light preferred: dawn, dusk, or warm interior sodium light.
  • Never crop a person's face directly at the frame edge.
  • Avoid: staged smiles at a laptop, generic city skylines, cool blue color grades.
  • Every photo gets a 1px gold hairline frame when placed in a document.
Halloway Studio · CONFIDENTIAL07
Visual Direction

Iconography

Line icons only — 1.5px stroke, no fills, rounded joins, single gold or ink color, 24px grid.

  • Always 1.5px stroke — never a filled glyph, never mixed with emoji.
  • Corners round at 2px radius; joins always rounded, never mitered.
  • Single color per icon: gold on dark, paper-ink on light.
  • 24×24 base grid, 2px optical padding on all sides.
