The Situation

The Challenge

Cascade Freight Brokers was booking loads the same way they had for a decade: phone tag with shippers, quotes built manually in a spreadsheet, and a website that existed but never converted a single lead. Dispatch was excellent. Growth was flat — not from a lack of freight in the market, but from a front door that quietly turned prospects away.

Average quote turnaround sat at 4 hours. Inbound leads that didn't get a same-day reply almost never booked. Nobody on the team had capacity to fix the funnel while also running dispatch.

Our Approach

01
Diagnose & Rebuild the Front Door
Audited the quote-to-book path, rebuilt the site around one conversion goal, and stood up a rate-quote form that routes straight to dispatch.
02
Automate the Repetitive Middle
Replaced manual quote-building with templated, auto-populated rate sheets and a follow-up sequence that fires without anyone touching it.
03
Layer On Outbound Growth
Built a verified-lead outbound motion targeting shippers in Cascade Freight Brokers's strongest lanes, paired with a brand system that made every touchpoint feel like one company.
