15 · Definition

Glossary

Net Revenue Retention

/net ˈrɛv.ə.nu rɪˈtɛn.ʃən/ · noun

The percentage of recurring revenue retained from existing customers over a period, including expansions and minus churn and downgrades. A figure above 100% means existing customers are growing faster than they’re leaving.

In Practice118% NRR means the existing base alone grew revenue 18% this year.
