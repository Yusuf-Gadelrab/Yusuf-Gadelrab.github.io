The Story

Why "earned."

I graduated valedictorian. Same year, I was walking off the football field as league MVP — receiver, running back, linebacker, whatever the team needed — and wrestling in the off-season. Two completely different rooms, the classroom and the huddle, but the same rule ran both: nothing gets handed to you, you put the work in first and let the result speak.

"Sef" is سيف — sayf, Arabic for sword. It was my first language, before English, before code. A sword isn't given to you — it's forged, then earned through use. That's the whole brand sitting inside one word.

I'm building KXNG SEF the same way I build everything else — in public, one drop at a time, no funding, no hype cycle. Just a CS student at SJSU shipping things and telling you exactly how it's going. Drop 001 is six pieces, first run, print-on-demand. Every piece on this rack is earned.

FAQ

Straight answers.

How long does shipping take?

Every piece is printed to order, so there's a production window of a few business days before anything ships — nothing exists until you order it. Transit time is added on top of that and gets shown at checkout with the shipping options.

What does سيف mean?

سيف is sayf, Arabic for sword. "Sef" has been my nickname since childhood, and said slow it lands on the same word.

KXNG (king) + SEF (sword) = a king who forged his own blade — earned rule, not inherited rule. Arabic is on the garments because it's my first language, not because it looks good in a print.

Returns and exchanges?

Because each item is printed individually for your order, there are no change-of-mind returns on a correctly printed piece — that's the tradeoff that keeps a no-inventory brand possible. Anything that arrives misprinted, damaged, or different from what you ordered gets replaced: email a photo within 30 days of delivery and a replacement ships at no cost to you.

How does the sizing run?

Standard unisex cut, S through 2XL, on the standard unisex chart in the size guide. Between two sizes, or want the boxier streetwear drape? Size up.

How do I wash it without wrecking the print?

Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Don't iron directly on the print, don't bleach, don't dry clean. Inside out on cold is the whole trick — that's what keeps a printed graphic sharp.
