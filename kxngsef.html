The Story

Why "earned."

I graduated valedictorian. Same year, I was walking off the football field as league MVP — receiver, running back, linebacker, whatever the team needed — and wrestling in the off-season. Two completely different rooms, the classroom and the huddle, but the same rule ran both: nothing gets handed to you, you put the work in first and let the result speak.

"Sef" is سيف — sayf, Arabic for sword. It was my first language, before English, before code. A sword isn't given to you — it's forged, then earned through use. That's the whole brand sitting inside one word.

I'm building KXNG SEF the same way I build everything else — in public, one drop at a time, no funding, no hype cycle. Just a CS student at SJSU shipping things and telling you exactly how it's going. Drop 001 is six pieces, first run, print-on-demand. Every piece on this rack is earned.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I actually buy this right now?

No. Checkout is not connected yet, so every button on this page goes to the waitlist instead of a payment page. No card details are taken and nothing is collected beyond the email you send. Nothing has been sold yet. The waitlist gets first access at the launch prices the moment the store opens.

How does the sizing run?

Standard unisex cut, sizes S through 2XL, on the standard unisex chart in the size guide on this page. If you are between two sizes, or you want the boxier streetwear drape, size up. Because every piece is made to order, a wrong size cannot be returned, so lay a shirt you already own flat and match the pit-to-pit figure before you order.

How long does production and shipping take?

Production runs about 2 to 7 business days, because the garment does not exist until you order it. Transit is added on top of that: roughly 3 to 5 business days inside the US and 10 to 20 business days internationally. A realistic US order is one to two weeks door to door. The figures shown at checkout for your address are the authoritative ones.

What are the garments actually made of?

Tees print on a Comfort Colors 1717 heavyweight garment-dyed blank, one print location. The hoodie is a Gildan 18500 pullover in a cotton and polyester fleece blend, also one print location. Black garments print in bone and gold ink, bone garments print in a single near-black ink. Exact fabric weights and print-area dimensions are confirmed at fulfilment rather than guessed at here.

Can I return it if I change my mind?

No. There are no change-of-mind returns on a correctly printed order, because the piece was made for you and cannot go back into stock that does not exist. That is the honest trade-off of a no-inventory brand. Anything that arrives misprinted, damaged, or different from what you ordered gets replaced free: email a photo within 30 days of delivery. The 14-day no-questions refund on this site covers digital products only, not made-to-order garments, which are governed by the Shipping and Returns page.

Do I pay import duties on an international order?

Yes, if your country charges them. Import duties, VAT and customs handling fees are set by your country, are not included in the checkout price, and are collected by the carrier or customs authority on delivery. None of that money comes to KXNG SEF.

How do I wash it without wrecking the print?

Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on the print, do not bleach, do not dry clean. Inside out on cold is the whole trick, and it is what keeps a printed graphic sharp.

What does سيف mean?

سيف is sayf, Arabic for sword. "Sef" has been my nickname since childhood, and said slowly it lands on the same word. KXNG (king) plus SEF (sword) reads as a king who forged his own blade — earned rule, not inherited rule. Arabic is on the garments because it is my first language, not because it looks good in a print.
