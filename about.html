Does he claim trading profits?

No. He publishes backtests and walk-forward results labelled as such, including the ones that went against him. Walk-forward testing invalidated most of the setups he had been trading: only the anchored-VWAP reclaim survived. An earlier, larger result on a far smaller sample then failed his own adversarial re-test and is retired — the defensible number is +0.117R over 4,933 trades across a 129-symbol, 10-year universe (95% CI +0.057 to +0.174), and most of even that is market drift rather than the signal itself. VCP came out breakeven; a gap and opening-range-breakout proxy came out at −0.28R and was dropped. None of it is financial advice.
