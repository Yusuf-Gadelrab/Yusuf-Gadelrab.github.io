Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026 · Applies to yusuf-gadelrab.github.io and all DHAHAB apps (TradeLog, Cut, Fire)

The short version

Your app data never leaves your device. The DHAHAB apps have no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, and no server of ours that receives your information. Everything you enter is stored in your own browser's local storage, on your device, under your control.

What the apps store, and where

  • TradeLog — the trades you log (dates, symbols, prices, notes) are saved only in your browser's localStorage.
  • Cut — your weight entries and goal are saved only in your browser's localStorage.
  • Fire — your tasks, check-offs, and streak history are saved only in your browser's localStorage.

Deleting your browser data, or removing the app from your home screen and clearing site data, permanently erases this information. We cannot recover it — because we never had it.

What we do not do

  • No cookies set by us, and no third-party analytics or advertising scripts
  • No sale, sharing, or transfer of personal information to anyone
  • No account creation, email capture, or profiling

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. That logging is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement; we do not receive or use it.

Purchases & outbound links

Products in the store are sold through third-party checkout providers (e.g., Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy). Payment details go directly to those processors under their own privacy policies — we never see your card information. Some pages may link to external sites (Instagram, LinkedIn, partner brands); their practices are their own.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or port — your data already lives only with you. If you have any privacy question or believe this policy is inaccurate, email yusuf.gadelrab06@gmail.com and you will get a reply within a reasonable time.

Children

The site and apps are not directed at children under 13 and collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything here changes (for example, if an app ever adds an online feature), this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date, before that change ships.
