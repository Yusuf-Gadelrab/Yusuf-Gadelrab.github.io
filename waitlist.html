FAQ

Common questions

What happens after I join a waitlist?

You get exactly one email when that specific thing launches, and early access before it goes public. Joining one list never subscribes you to the others, and there is no newsletter.

Will my email be sold or shared?

No. Emails are used only to notify you about the thing you signed up for. There is no list rental, no ad retargeting, and no third-party sharing. Reply once to any message to be removed.

Do waitlist members get a discount?

Where a product is paid, waitlist members get access before the public and the lowest price offered. For the KXNG SEF drop that means an early-bird tier; for the trading cohort it means founding-cohort pricing.

Which things are free?

The DHAHAB Circle community, the DIRA security scanner, and all of the installable apps and calculators are free. FreightDesk, the KXNG SEF drop, and the trading cohort are paid.
