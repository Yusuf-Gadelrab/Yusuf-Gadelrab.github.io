FAQ

Common questions

What apps does Yusuf Gadelrab offer for free?

TradeLog, an R-multiple trading journal; Cut, a weight and body-recomposition tracker; Fire, a daily execution-ritual checklist; and GRAMPA, a big-button phone helper for older adults with step-by-step guides and scam protection. All four install as offline progressive web apps. The site also hosts free calculators for trading risk and freight brokerage, a visa planner, and an application tracker.

Do these apps need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. There is no signup and no backend. Once installed, the apps work fully offline and store data in your device's local browser storage.

How do I install these apps on an iPhone or Android?

Open the app in Safari on iOS and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. They then launch like native apps and run offline.

Is my data collected or sold?

No. There is no telemetry, no analytics on the app data, and no server to send it to. Everything stays on your device. Details are in the privacy policy at https://yusuf-gadelrab.github.io/privacy.html.
