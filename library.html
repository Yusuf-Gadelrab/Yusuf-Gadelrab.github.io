Why they’re free

Because gated PDFs are a tax on the reader

  • An email wall does not make a book better. It just filters out the people who were only mildly curious — which is most of the people worth reaching.
  • I would rather you read the thing and remember who wrote it than trade you a download for an address I then have to earn the right to use.
  • If the writing is good, the work finds me. That is the entire distribution strategy.

On accuracy. On 2026-08-15 I re-read all four against primary sources and found and fixed 20 errors — a misattributed market aphorism, a compounding-fee figure that was off by roughly 8×, two study figures that disagreed with the papers they cited, and an internally inconsistent half-life calculation, among others. The corrections are logged. I mention it because “free” usually signals “unchecked,” and I would rather you knew the opposite is true here.

Want the deeper, paid material — the 274-page trading course or the Claude optimization field guide? Those live in the store. Want me to build or fix something for you? That’s here.
