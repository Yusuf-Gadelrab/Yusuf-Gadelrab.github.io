Things that are true about me and sound made up

① My AI agent went rogue and emailed 20 companies

Not a bit. A background agent I built read a command out of my own to-do file at 05:39 and cold-emailed twenty freight brokerages before I woke up. The rule it broke was written at the top of its own instructions file. I now make my software prove a human is at the keyboard before it's allowed to send anything. Confused deputy, meet confused deputy's author.

② I've spent $0.00 on AI APIs

Every model I ship runs on this laptop. Not out of principle — out of being a student. The stack costs nothing and my only rate limit is thermal. People assume there's a monthly bill hiding somewhere. There is: it's electricity, and my landlord pays it.

③ I lost 60 lb and gained 46 charts

260 to 200, documented publicly. Then I wrote a trading book and generated all thirty-nine of its plates from Python instead of drawing them. Turns out the same trait causes both.

④ I was a valedictorian and a league MVP in the same year

Wide receiver, running back, outside linebacker, and the graduation speech. Peaked at 18 and have been recovering by shipping software since.

⑤ My visa expires because of my birthday

TD status ends at 21. Not a rule I broke, just arithmetic slowly happening to me. I built a web app to plan the paperwork, because of course I did. It's on this site. It's the least funny page here and by far the most useful to me.

⑥ I named my habit app after a Japanese word and ranked it like karate

Tenth kyu to tenth dan, and a weekly demon with a health bar that you damage by going outside in the morning. Sunlight does 12 damage. Cold plunge does 10. This is the most effective thing I have ever built for myself.

Engineering truths, unfortunately universal

◷ "It works on my machine"

It does. It is also the only machine. These two facts are related and I've made peace with exactly one of them.

◷ The bug was in the prompt

I asked a model to keep titles under 55 characters. It wrote 6,700 characters of arithmetic counting them and never produced a title. I stopped asking it to count.

◷ Empty response, no error

A local model returned nothing at all for three debugging rounds. It had spent its entire token budget thinking and never got to the answer. Deeply relatable behaviour.

◷ The tests passed. The screenshot did not.

Forty-one automated checks green, and the page was rendering at thumbnail size on a phone the whole time. Look at the thing. Always look at the thing.
